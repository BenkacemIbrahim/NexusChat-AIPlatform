# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

This project is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas, not people.
- Give actionable technical feedback.
- Accept responsibility and correct mistakes.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or abusive language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, or PRs that violate this code of conduct.
